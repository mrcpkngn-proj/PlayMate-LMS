🚀 About PlayMate

Welcome to PlayMate LMS!

PlayMate is a gamified learning management system designed to make learning more interactive, engaging, and enjoyable.

🎮 What can you do?

🏫 Classrooms
Join and manage learning classrooms where teachers can organize lessons, quizzes, assignments, and announcements.

📝 Interactive Learning
Complete quizzes and assignments while tracking your learning progress.

📊 Learning Analytics
Students can monitor their performance, while teachers can view classroom and student performance.

🚀 Meet A.L.E.C.

AI-enhanced Learning Environment Core

A.L.E.C. is PlayMate's learning companion designed to provide students and teachers with useful learning insights and classroom information.
